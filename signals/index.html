Provider status and public measurement · 5 October 2026

Service and task signals point to different explanations.

A Work Mode incident is under recovery monitoring. Opus 5.5’s public score rose from its 2 October low; no tracker has reported a confirmed comparison result.

Do not read this as a broad model decline. Check the service surface, then the model that answered, then the same task after recovery.

Availability: OpenAI Work Mode

OpenAI’s incident record lists elevated Work Mode errors from 07:03 UTC on 5 October. It said the issue was mitigated and recovery monitored at 09:50 UTC; ChatGPT Work and Codex in ChatGPT Desktop were listed as affected. The status page does not report answer quality or root cause.

Safety and routing: check the answer label

Anthropic’s help article says some safety-flagged Opus 5.5 requests can fall back to Opus 5 or Opus 4.8. It says Claude displays a switch notice and labels the responding model, and that the model selector stays on the fallback until changed. This is documented product behavior for narrow categories, not a general capability measurement.

Opus 5.5 score movement

BridgeBench shows 96.5% of launch power on 4 October, up from its 94.2% low on 2 October. The latest reading is inside its 90–110% band; the board shows five tests. The composite combines task score, tokens, and cost, while its actual tasks and weights are private.

Baseline status

LiveNerf reports 12/30 daily collections, ten baseline days complete, and two follow-up days without a comparison result. Its first possible decision is around 24 October. Marginlab still says its baseline is collecting and detection paused; the page is marked last updated 29 September.

Community lead

A user’s Godot report says a prompt and image set produced different artifacts across two dates. It was not independently reproduced. A separate usage-meter thread asks for matched peak/off-peak runs; no controlled meter data is posted.

Next check

  • Recheck OpenAI status before attributing a Work or Desktop failure to a model.
  • For Claude, record the per-response model label and any fallback notice.
  • Replay a preserved task only after recovery, with the same files, tools, effort, and route where visible.
  • Wait for a completed LiveNerf comparison and keep its sensitivity and serving-path limits visible.
Checked 5 October 2026 at 12:13 UTC. Community reports are leads; monitor readiness and service status do not establish model capability.
