Provider records and repeated tests · 1 October 2026

Space Pages still showed degraded status. The model test is still collecting.

A live availability signal and a future capability comparison must not share one verdict.

Broad deployed-model capability decline remains unproven. OpenAI’s status record still showed Monitoring / Degraded performance for ChatGPT Space Pages. Separately, a public Opus 5.5 test had not completed its launch baseline.

A time series has started, but no comparison is ready

LiveNerf’s public repository reported seven of 30 daily collections through 30 September; its first ten days are the baseline. It compares Opus 5.5 on a Claude Code subscription using a calibrated 78-item panel and an Opus 5 control on GPQA items. The preregistered rule requires a 99% interval to exclude zero in two consecutive ten-day windows, an effect of at least three points, and no matching control movement. No post-baseline result was posted at this check; the first possible call is around 24 October.

Another board is not an equivalent measurement

BridgeBench Nerf Bench last displayed tests dated 27 September for two models: Opus 5.5 at 99.2% and GPT-6 Astra at 102.8% of their launch baselines, within a stated 90–110% band. Its method page describes a private composite of performance, tokens, and cost. Without its task and sample details, those scores cannot be recomputed or combined with LiveNerf.

Next checks

  • Check OpenAI’s Space Pages page for a resolved state and any explanation of the recovery sequence.
  • Wait for LiveNerf’s complete baseline and comparison windows; do not read an interim baseline as a regression result.
  • Keep any alert tied to a task, tested surface, valid-run coverage, comparison rule, and uncertainty.
