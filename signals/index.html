Open questions

  • Can provider-side logs reconcile the 9 September lockouts with the local meters and exact limit buckets?
  • Does the WebSocket failure affect desktop only, image-heavy tasks, specific models, or a shared transport path?
  • Can the Astra field panel expose stable task and route slices so outcome and cost movement can be compared?
  • Does the false-completion pattern reproduce in a clean matched task with final-artifact and server-side completion state?
Current boundary: “incident confirmed,” “transport failure,” “quota lead,” “safety interruption,” “baseline collecting,” and “capability regression” remain different states.
