Provider status and public measurement · 6 October 2026

Service incidents recovered; a new quality complaint remains unresolved.

OpenAI reported several recent service recoveries. A user’s GPT-5.6 Sol High report appeared after those updates, but lacks the task receipt needed to test whether the symptom persisted.

Do not read a status recovery as a model test. OpenAI’s live dashboard showed all systems operational at our check. It is not an account-level quality measure.

Same-day ChatGPT complaint

A 6 October post reports that GPT-5.6 Sol High felt weaker than two days earlier, with context and reasoning symptoms. It was posted after the recovery updates, but has no task definition, transcript, output, exact client, route, or verified model identity. Replies include both similar complaints and users who say their service works normally. This is one report of possible continued symptoms after service recoveries; it does not identify cause or prevalence.

Opus 5.5 trackers

BridgeBench still displays 96.5% of launch power on 4 October, inside its 90–110% band; its score combines task performance, tokens, and cost. LiveNerf last reports 12/30 daily collections by 5 October, with no post-baseline result. These monitors cover Claude and do not test the new GPT-5.6 report.

Next check

  • Match a complaint to the exact product and incident window.
  • Record selected model, effort, surface/client, displayed responding model, task/context, output, and correction work.
  • After recovery, replay the same task against a dated baseline before switching.
  • Keep community reports, provider status, and repeated task results in separate evidence lanes.
