Provider status and public measurement · 7 October 2026

Two OpenAI product issues were marked resolved; no model decline is established.

One issue affected new ChatGPT Work desktop threads on a named app version. A separate issue affected Workspace Agent responses. Neither measures model capability or explains the 6 October ChatGPT complaint.

For the Work desktop issue, update the app if affected. For a missing Workspace Agent response, check service status and retry after recovery. For a weak completed answer, preserve and replay the same task.

Quality report remains unverified

A 6 October r/ChatGPT post reports context and reasoning symptoms on a selected GPT-5.6 Sol High setting. It has no task, transcript, output, exact client, route, or verified final model. Replies include similar complaints and normal-use counterreports. Nothing in the two 7 October incident records links them to this complaint.

Opus 5.5 trackers

BridgeBench lists 96.5% of launch power on 4 October within its 90–110% band; its measure combines task performance, tokens, and cost. LiveNerf reports 13/30 days as of 6 October and no result. Its closed issue #9 reports a windowing concern, with no linked resolution visible. Its missing result is not evidence of no change.

Next check

  • For a Work desktop thread issue, update the app and confirm the affected function works.
  • For an absent agent response, first verify status and execution; do not score a missing answer as low-quality output.
  • For a completed quality complaint, record surface, client, settings, context, displayed model, output, and correction effort; replay after recovery.
  • Keep status, user reports, and repeated task outcomes in separate evidence lanes.
