How to read a monitor

Compare its timestamp, task set, serving surface, baseline, metric, detector maturity, and missing runs. A current date does not mean full product coverage; a detector that has not completed its arming window has not tested for a sustained change.

For a completed poor answer, repeat a safe task with the same prompt, surface, model, effort, context, and settings. For a failed request, check status and the affected tool or connector first. Keep correctness, safety, completion, latency, cost, quota, and correction time separate.
