Provider status and public measurement · 3 October 2026

One board shows a lower Opus 5.5 score; the fixed comparison is not ready.

BridgeBench’s 94.2% reading is inside its own variance band and combines performance, token use, and cost.

Call: do not label the movement a confirmed model regression. The score is sparse, composite, and not independently reproducible from the published page.

Opus 5.5 board reading

BridgeBench’s board shows a 2 October result of 94.2% of launch power. That is −5.8% versus launch and −9.6% versus its 1 October test. The board calls 90–110% normal variance and displays four tests since launch. Its method description combines task score, tokens, and cost; it keeps the actual tasks and weights private. The result page does not show sample count or uncertainty.

Fixed-panel test is still collecting

LiveNerf’s latest dated progress note is from 1 October and reports 8/10 baseline days. The results table remains “baseline (collecting)” with no post-baseline comparison. Its preregistered rule requires two later 10-day windows, a 99% interval excluding zero in both windows, an effect of at least three points, and no matching change in the control arm. The first possible call is around 24 October. This suite tests one subscription path and panel, not all Claude products or user workflows.

Availability check

OpenAI’s status page said systems were fully operational, and Anthropic’s status page showed listed services operational at the 3 October check. Aggregate status can help rule in or out a declared incident; it does not show individual account health or completed-answer quality.

Next check

  • Wait for LiveNerf’s post-baseline data and read its control, sample error rate, and stated detectable effect.
  • Ask whether BridgeBench publishes sample size, uncertainty, and the components of its next result.
  • If a familiar task fails, preserve the exact prompt, surface, settings, route if visible, output, and correction work; replay the same task after status recovery.
Checked 3 October 2026 at 12:10 UTC. A composite score, a task series, and provider status do not measure the same outcome.
