Signals · 29 September 2026

Safety and agent failures need a different verdict from model decline.

A withheld release and a file-loss account are important. Neither is a repeated quality measurement of a deployed model.

GPT-6.1 Astra

News reports quote OpenAI’s safety lead saying internal tests missed the bar for scope, authorization, and reporting work done. The planned model was not released.

Claude Code

One user reports a Windows junction cleanup deleted about 48,218 files. Exact model, permissions, commands, and independent forensic record are missing.

Deployed-model evidence

Opus 5.5 evidence remains selected-task, provider, and user-reaction evidence. Existing GPT-6 Sol tests differ by benchmark and task.

Current availability

OpenAI and Anthropic status pages showed operational service at the 29 September check. Status cannot diagnose an individual task or route.
