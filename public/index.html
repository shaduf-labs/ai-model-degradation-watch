Current call · 9 September 2026 · 06:10 UTC
NO ACTIVE DECLARED INCIDENT AT LAST CHECK

The newest lead is a route, not a model collapse.

The strongest new evidence points to serving, access, and safety behavior: what users receive can change even when a model label stays the same. A Google developer report may show a long-context Pro-to-Flash-Lite switch, but it is not confirmed. Broad capability decline is still unproven.

Confidence: high for declared and provider-documented behavior · medium for the narrow Gemini route lead · low for a general capability decline.

What changed since the last check? Provider documentation now makes two confounders explicit: Anthropic describes safety-triggered fallback from Fable 5.1, and OpenAI describes plan-, model-, effort-, and task-dependent allowance. A Google AI Studio forum report adds a technically detailed route hypothesis above roughly 12,600 input tokens. The report needs reproduction and provider confirmation.

The answer in one screen

Strongest new lead

Verify what served

The reported final stream changes from Pro to Flash Lite on long prompts. If real, that is a route or product event, not proof that Pro weights weakened.

Documented behavior

Safety and budget matter

Fallback, plan, model, reasoning, Fast mode, task, and allowance can alter quality, speed, and cost without a broad capability decline.

Capability check

No broad regression proof

Codex is nominal on a scoped tracker; Claude Code’s detector is collecting a new baseline. Neither tests every consumer surface.

When your task feels worse

  1. Record provider, surface, visible model label, final served model or fallback, plan, effort, context size, task, and time.
  2. Check status, release notes, access rules, safety notices, and usage limits.
  3. Replay the same task in a fresh thread or stable test; count corrections, missing context, interruptions, latency, refusal, and task cost.
  4. Switch only after matched failures repeat or your production metric moves. Treat an unverified route report as a lead, not a verdict.

Evidence worth opening

  • Google developer report: a possible final-stream Pro-to-Flash-Lite mismatch on long prompts.
  • Anthropic fallback guide and OpenAI usage guide: designed routes, access, and allowance are part of the test.
  • Marginlab Codex tracker is nominal; its Claude Code tracker says baseline collection is in progress.
  • OpenAI history, Claude status, and Grok status show the declared service layer at the check.
