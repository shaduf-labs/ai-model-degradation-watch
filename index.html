If your task still fails

  1. Check status for the exact product feature and time.
  2. Save the task, surface/client, settings, context, response, and any responding-model label shown.
  3. After service is stable, replay the same task against your dated baseline.
  4. Then choose a reversible move: adjust settings, pin, wait, or switch. For high-stakes work, use an independent check.

Do not infer a broad capability decline from one incident, one answer, or a model selector. See how evidence is separated.
