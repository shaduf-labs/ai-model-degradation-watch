How we read · Updated 2 October 2026

Opinion, status, and model output need separate measures.

A daily sentiment drop can prompt a check. It cannot replace a matched task result.

1 · Availability

Did the surface work?

Check the dated incident and exact feature. OpenAI marked Space Pages resolved at 23:44 UTC on 1 October, after a warning of slow or timed-out results earlier that day.

2 · Opinion

What did people report?

Show the source, sampled audience, date window, scoring method, and uncertainty. A Reddit opinion index measures discussion, not what a model can do.

3 · Task quality

Did the same work change?

Replay a valid completed task using the same model, surface, route, context, and settings. Compare the artifact and corrections against a dated baseline.

The sentiment monitor says a scoring-model change on 27 September moved historical series by up to 6.5 points. LiveNerf’s preregistration requires two ten-day post-baseline windows, a 99% interval excluding zero, an effect of at least three points, and no matching control movement. Do not combine these into one quality score.

Minimum decision receipt

task · time · surface · plan · requested and served model · route · settings · request and response · artifact · errors · corrections · allowance · baseline · valid-run count

Share only what is safe. Do not submit credentials, private account data, or sensitive prompts.

Evidence grades

  • Provider status: evidence of declared service conditions and recovery, not task-quality evidence.
  • Community report: early evidence of user experience; not a representative sample or causal explanation by itself.
  • Sentiment monitor: a time-windowed view of discussion whose feed and grader can shift.
  • Repeated task test: evidence within its task, served identity, surface, settings, baseline, valid-run coverage, and uncertainty.
  • Broad degradation claim: needs repeated, comparable results across deployed surfaces and a defined population.
