Method · evidence separation

We test the explanation before naming degradation.

The standing question is whether popular AI models are getting worse right now. The method separates capability from outages, routing, product changes, safety behavior, price, access limits, and anecdotes.

Evidence order

  1. State the surface. Name Chat, Work, Codex, API, client, route, release boundary, and incident state.
  2. Check identity. Record requested, served, request, server, native, client, and final model attribution when available.
  3. Match the task. Keep prompt, context, tools, effort, system instructions, harness, judge, and artifact review fixed.
  4. Repeat. Record n, run-to-run spread, coverage, confidence, and a time-matched control.
  5. Separate outcomes. Measure speed, allowance, cost, correction burden, and accepted artifact independently.
  6. Choose a reversible action. Retry, wait, audit, pin, stop, or switch only after the evidence lane is clear.
