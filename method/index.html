How we read · updated 17 September 2026

Count the work window and served system, not just the model name.

An allowance shock can be real without proving a weight change. The receipt must show the path and the outcome.

New diagnostic: label the lane before choosing the cause: capability, access, allowance economics, surface, safety, benchmark, or anecdote.

The comparison receipt

Identity

What was requested and served?

Requested model, provider-reported effective model, fallback, service tier, client, runtime, and date.

Capacity

What could the account use?

Plan, workspace, capacity event, errors, allowance, meter, ledger, and route or region if available.

Context

What was loaded?

Prompt, prior turns, skills, instructions, tool schemas, context growth, effort, and completion boundary.

Outcome

What work survived?

Tool lifecycle, final artifact, self-check, corrections, elapsed time, cache, output, and accepted result.

Evidence ladder

  1. Provider records: declared incidents, releases, access rules, limits, and recovery.
  2. Detailed reports: task sequence and failure detail, with account and missing-control limits.
  3. Matched tests: capability claims only when identity, task, context, settings, surface, sample, and baseline align.
  4. Benchmarks: task set, harness, agent, effort, tool stack, call budget, artifact rubric, cost, and date.
  5. Runtime/account telemetry: throughput, context, cache, or meter movement; without outcome it cannot show quality.
  6. Community evidence: lived friction and demand; selection blocks prevalence and causal inference.

Current upgrade threshold

To upgrade the allowance lead, reproduce the 73% report with a bounded task, route receipt, context record, control model, and provider ledger. To upgrade the Astra quality lead, replicate the same task across accounts after the capacity event with effective-model provenance and artifact scoring. To call broad capability decline, show repeated negative movement across providers and surfaces on a stable task set after access, routing, context, safety, and product explanations are checked.
