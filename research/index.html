A capable model can still be a failed product.

The newest reports make the boundary visible. A provider incident can be confirmed while a reader’s account remains unexplained; a desktop stream can fail while the web surface works; and a strong field outcome can coexist with expensive, interrupted sessions.

Reading rule: “incident confirmed,” “account deduction explained,” “agent failure,” “safety interruption,” and “capability regression” are different states. Do not collapse them into one score.

What turns a report into useful evidence?

1

Verify the act

Did the tool run? Did the final artifact exist? Did the stream end cleanly or only say it did?

2

Pin usable access

Record model, surface, plan, effort, route, transport, safety path, quota window, and cost.

3

Run the matched check

Replay the same task and compare success, corrections, interruptions, latency, and cost against a stable baseline.
