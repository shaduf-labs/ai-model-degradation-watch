Space Pages still showed degraded status after a recovery update.

Separating a current service incident from the long-term question of model quality.

OpenAI · Space Pages

Recovery status changed

At 10:34 UTC on 30 September, OpenAI said impacted services had recovered. At 15:00 it said mitigation was applied and recovery was being monitored. The status still showed Monitoring / Degraded performance at the 1 October check.

Anthropic · 1 October

No incident listed

The provider status page showed all systems operational and no incidents reported today. Status is not a completed-task quality comparison.

LiveNerf · Opus 5.5

Baseline still collecting

The repository reported seven of 30 daily collections by 30 September, with no post-baseline result yet. Its test runs through Claude Code on a subscription, not every provider surface.

One r/chatgptplus user reported an error that remained after refreshing a Space. It is a single post, not a prevalence estimate, but it matches the feature in OpenAI’s status entry.

Two monitors, different questions

BridgeBench’s Nerf Bench showed Opus 5.5 at 99.2% and GPT-6 Astra at 102.8% relative to launch in its 27 September snapshot, inside its stated ±10% range. The board counts only two models and its method uses a private composite. LiveNerf exposes more of its preregistered protocol but has no comparison result yet. Neither can be treated as a verdict on all models or users. Read the evidence comparison.

Preserve before retrying

  1. Exact timestamp, product surface, account tier, and region.
  2. Request, error, response, task artifact, and tool/request IDs that can be shared safely.
  3. Model label and route, plan access, effort, settings, context, and usage budget.
  4. After service recovery, replay the same task and score valid outputs against a dated baseline.
