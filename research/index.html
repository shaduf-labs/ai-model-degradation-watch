Community reports · 26 September 2026

Reports are intake, not a denominator.

Keep the task, surface, route, effort, artifact, and competing explanation attached to each report. A vivid complaint can reveal a replay lead without proving prevalence.

Negative coding report

Bug Hunt reports GPT-6 Sol at 29.3/105 versus GPT-5.6 Sol at 43.5/105 on maximum means. See the board and method limits.

Subscription-value report

A high-engagement r/codex thread connects quality, API price, allowance, and usable work. It is self-selected.

Resolved access incident

OpenAI's status record documents a full Codex outage. That supports retry or wait, not a model verdict.

What to capture

  • Exact task and final artifact, including omissions, corrections, and acceptance.
  • Surface, client, release boundary, requested and served model, route, execution class, and effort.
  • Tools, context, hidden I/O, allowance, warnings, stop state, cost, and incident status.
  • Control task, repeated runs, harness and judge, and the outcome after retry or switch.

Counterreports matter

Braintrust's broader API evaluation puts GPT-6 Sol and GPT-5.6 Sol nearly tied, while GPT-6 Luna is slightly ahead. That does not erase the negative coding slice. It prevents both sources from being read as a universal answer. Read the full comparison.
