Community reports · 27 September 2026

Community intake can find symptoms. It cannot set prevalence.

A useful report records the task and the system that ran it. Keep the source date visible and check how fresh its linked summaries are.

Current Claude report format

The r/ClaudeAI performance hub asks for prompts, responses, platform, time, and screenshots. A moderator says the hub often draws the subreddit’s highest traffic but gives no figures.

Check the linked data date

The hub links a category report log, but its displayed hourly summary is dated 21 September. A new discussion post does not make the linked measurements current.

Current model evidence

The 27 September report explains why RoboHarm’s safety outcomes do not establish model degradation.

What to capture

  • Exact task and final artifact, including omissions, corrections, and acceptance.
  • Surface, client, release boundary, requested and served model, route, execution class, and effort.
  • Tools, context, hidden I/O, allowance, warnings, stop state, cost, and incident status.
  • Control task, repeated runs, harness and judge, the outcome after retry or switch, and the date the underlying data was updated.

Use reports as leads

Community complaints can show what users notice and which task to replay. A moderator’s traffic claim does not give a denominator, and the linked log’s latest visible summary may lag behind its parent thread. The 26 September comparison keeps a negative coding result separate from an API near-tie; the new safety result is a separate physical-agent setup.
