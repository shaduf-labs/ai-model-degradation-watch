What this changes

The right comparison is now an assembled system: model, tool stack, context, surface, account, and artifact rubric. Artificial Analysis gives Astra a real positive and negative split. Astra-26 shows that tool choice can move the output even when the model and task are held broadly constant. Current Codex reports show that a good model can still fail to deliver usable work when access or tool state breaks.

The reader protocol

Preserve the smallest failing task and expected artifact. Pin the visible and effective model, route, runtime, effort, context, tools, and plan. Count the tool lifecycle. Save the final artifact and correction sequence. Record allowance, cache, retries, elapsed time, and cost. Then classify the result as capability, context, tool, surface, access, meter, or still unclear.
